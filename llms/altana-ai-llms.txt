# Altana: The AI-Powered Network for Trusted Trade

> Altana's AI-powered network empowers governments and businesses to build a more resilient and secure global economy while keeping trade flowing. Our mission: fix globalization.

Fragility, mistrust, and geopolitics have brought the free trade era to an end. Now, in a new era of geopolitical competition and trade wars, trade and supply chain networks are prone to constant disruption. The changing economic landscape requires a new global trade architecture built on trusted networks.

Altana connects the world's largest logistics providers, businesses, and their suppliers with government agencies like US Customs and Border Protection around a shared source of product information. Businesses connect to Altana to automate and navigate global trade and tariff complexity, and to build supply chain resilience. Government agencies connect to Altana to design and enforce trade and economic security policies.
